About ICEP

A student community for advanced space propulsion.

Imperial students from every discipline, united by a passion for electric propulsion and deep-space exploration.

  • Learn through theory sessions, team training and shared research.
  • Build skills in design, manufacturing and assembly.
  • Simulate with Julia programming and engineering analysis.

Our first project

In development

A 1 kW argon
Hall-effect thruster.

We are developing our first argon Hall-effect thruster, taking it from design and simulation towards manufacture and ground testing.

Materials for the first thruster have been obtained. Through an iterative design process, we are developing the thruster components and supporting systems needed for testing at the Imperial Plasma Propulsion Laboratory.

Project scope

Design, build, test and learn, from our first thruster to future spacecraft.

Thruster development

  • Design reviews, component sizing and procurement.
  • Manufacture and assemble the thruster and supporting systems.
  • Prepare for a ground demonstration.

Mission & systems studies

  • Define conceptual missions and propulsion requirements.
  • Study spacecraft integration and how our systems could scale.

Student learning & outreach

  • Practical training, shared research and project work.
  • Connect students across disciplines and with other propulsion and CubeSat teams.

Longer-term ambitions

Build experience today. Explore the propulsion and missions of tomorrow.

Beyond our first thruster

  • Refine Hall-thruster designs and explore different propellants.
  • Build experience towards propulsion for small satellites.

Humanity beyond Earth

  • Explore human spaceflight, conceptual spacecraft and interplanetary missions.
  • Study the propulsion and power systems those missions could need.

From ground tests to orbit

  • Work towards electric propulsion on a student CubeSat.
  • A future dream: an electrically propelled payload alongside Imperial College London Rocketry.

Collaboration and competitions

  • Plan to enter student electric propulsion competitions as our hardware develops.
  • In talks alongside the Student Electric Propulsion Consortium about wider opportunities, including R2S EP.

Our teams

Find your place in ICEP.

Two main branches, working together on one thruster. Explore the roles below, with part-time Marketing opportunities alongside your technical or simulation work.

ICEP

Design & build

Technical

Turn mission requirements into thruster designs and physical hardware.

Systems and Management

Define a conceptual spacecraft mission and use it to set thruster requirements. Coordinate the subteams, track progress and report our work to stakeholders and academics.

Computer-Aided Design

Size the thruster, develop its conceptual geometry and create detailed 3D models in Fusion 360. Turn engineering requirements into a design the team can manufacture.

Manufacturing, Assembly, Integration and Testing

Develop the magnets, cathode and anode. Manufacture and assemble the hardware, integrate its systems and prepare it for testing.

Model & analyse

Simulations

Understand the design through our own code and established engineering software.

In-house simulations

Build modules and workflows around PEPAPIC using Julia and particle-in-cell modelling. Develop our own tools to connect thruster designs with simulations.

Julia & PEPAPIC

Commercial simulations

Use established software to model the magnetic circuit, analyse thermal behaviour and study gas flow through the anode.

FEMM & Ansys

Marketing
