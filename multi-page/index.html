About ICEP

A student community for advanced space propulsion.

Imperial students from every discipline, united by a passion for electric propulsion and deep-space exploration.

  • Learn through theory sessions, team training and shared research.
  • Build skills in design, manufacturing and assembly.
  • Simulate with Julia programming and engineering analysis.

No previous Hall-thruster knowledge required.

Funded by Imperial’s Department of Aeronautics.
